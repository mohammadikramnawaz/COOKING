Method

Marinate the mutton

Mix together

  • Mutton
  • Yogurt
  • Half of the Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1/2 tbsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • Salt

Marinate for 1-2 hours (Overnight is even better)

Cook the onions

Heat oil in heavy bottomed pot or pressure cooker

Add
  • Bay leaves
  • Cinnamom
  • Cardamoms
  • Cloves
  • Cumin seeds

Once fragrant, add sliced onions and cook until deep golden-brown

Make the masala

Add the remaining Ginger-Garlic paste and saute for 2 minutes

Add tomatoes and cook until soft and oil seperates

Mix in

  • Turmeric
  • Coriander powder
  • Cumin powder
  • Kashmiri chilli powder
  • Salt

Cook for 3-5 minutes

Cook The mutton

Add the marinated mutton

Cook on medium-high heat for 10-15 minutes, stirring often until the meat is well coated and lightly browned

Pressure cook

Add about 2 cups hot water

Pressure cook:

  • Goat meat: 5-6 whistles, then simmer 15-20 minutes
  • Without a pressure cooker: Simmer covered for 2 hours, stirring occasionally, until tender

Finish

Once the mutton is tender:

  • Simmer uncovered for 5-10 minutes to thicken the gravy
  • Stir in garam masala
  • Add chopped coriander
  • Finish with a squeeze of lemon juice if desired
