Ingredients

For the Mutton

  1. 1kg Mutton (Medium Pieces)
  2. 2tbsp Sunflower Oil
  3. 2 Medium sized onions (Finely sliced)
  4. 2 Large tomatoes (Finely chopped)
  5. 1/2 cup fresh yogurt (Well whisked)
  6. 2tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  7. 2-3 Green chillies (Optional)

Whole spices

  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 4 green cardamoms
  • 4 cloves
  • 1tsp cumin seeds

Ground spices

  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1-2 tsp kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp hot red chilli powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Garnish

  • Fresh coriander leaves, Chopped
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
